The generations of Genesis

“In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.” (Gen 1:1)

Names, ages, places, events and verses from the King James Version. Tap a name to open its line, tap KJV verse to read the text, or switch Events off for the bare tree.

Blue names trace the line from God through Adam, Seth, Noah, Shem, Abraham and Isaac to Jacob. G is the generation counted from Adam.

Toledot headings in the KJV: 2:4; 5:1; 6:9; 10:1; 11:10; 11:27; 25:12; 25:19; 36:1; 37:2. Event headings follow the chapter divisions of the KJV. Text checked against the Project Gutenberg KJV.
