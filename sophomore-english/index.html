04

Every Meeting

The same two hours, in the same order, every week. You will always know what is coming, so you can spend your attention on the English rather than on the schedule.

Hour 1

Conversational Practice

  • Warm-up questions on this week’s theme.
  • Speaking in pairs and small groups, with partners changing week to week.
  • A short individual turn in front of the group.
  • Collect the words you needed but did not have.
Hour 2

E-book Practice

  • Work through this week’s unit in the e-book.
  • Listening and reading tasks done here in class, under time.
  • Check answers together, then find where in the text each answer was.
  • Carry the unit’s language back into next week’s first hour.
