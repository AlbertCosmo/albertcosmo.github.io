The knowledge tree of Exodus

“And God said unto Moses, I AM THAT I AM.” (Exod 3:14)

The story, people, places, plagues, laws, tabernacle, feasts and names of God in the King James Version, arranged as one tree. Tap a branch to open it, tap KJV verse to read the text, or switch Events off for the bare outline.

Blue entries trace the story from the house of bondage to the glory of the LORD filling the tabernacle. Grey labels give chapters, or the order of stops, plagues and commandments.

Every verse and quotation is checked against the KJV text of Exodus; words the KJV prints in italics are set in plain type. The commandments are numbered in the common Protestant order; Exodus itself calls them only “the ten commandments” (34:28). Companion to The generations of Genesis.
