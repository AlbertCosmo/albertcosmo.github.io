The knowledge tree of Ruth

“whither thou goest, I will go; and where thou lodgest, I will lodge” (Ruth 1:16)

A study guide for learners to the four chapters of Ruth in the King James Version: the story with KJV excerpts, the people and places, old words explained, the customs behind the plot, themes, and questions to discuss. Tap a branch to open it; the buttons show or hide all excerpts and notes.

Blue entries trace the story chapter by chapter, with the key verses printed under each scene. In the excerpts, words in italics were added by the KJV translators to complete the English.

Every excerpt and quotation is checked against the KJV text of Ruth and the books it points to. Notes on Hebrew words, customs, places and seasons are background for learners and are not part of the KJV. Companion to The generations of Genesis and The knowledge tree of Exodus.
