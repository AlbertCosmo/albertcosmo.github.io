04

Every Meeting

The same three hours, in the same order, every week. You will always know what is coming, so you can spend your attention on the English rather than on the schedule.

Hour 1

Speaking & Listening

  • Warm-up questions on this week’s theme.
  • A full two-minute individual long turn, recorded.
  • Listen back, then deliver it a second time.
  • Collect the words you needed but did not have.
Hour 2

Reading

  • Read the passage here in class, under time.
  • Check answers together.
  • Find where in the text each answer was.
  • Match the words in the question to the words in the text.
Hour 3

Writing

  • Take the prompt apart together.
  • Write under time.
  • Scripts returned marked within the week.
  • Every script rewritten once.
