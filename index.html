About

I am a Doctoral Research Fellow in Comparative Literature and Cross-Cultural Studies at Fu Jen Catholic University, where I also serve as a university lecturer. My work centers on literature, language education, identity, and the role of narrative in cross-cultural understanding.

Previously, I taught English and Vietnamese at Dalat University in Vietnam and coordinated high-profile international academic and diplomatic visits. My research and teaching are grounded in a commitment to empathy, rigorous scholarship, and the transformative power of education.
