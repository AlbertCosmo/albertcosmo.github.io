How to read the stats (plain language)

Text arm Gemini’s pooled r≈+0.21 vs human PleasantOther means shared variance is still modest (~4% if you square it). BERT near zero means classical per-segment sentiment is not a continuous film-emotion labeler here. Multi-item maps show models latch onto valence/social language more than pure bodily fear dynamics.
Brain arm Occipital “null” is strong: the decoder can recover planted signals but not real valence. Affective DECODES on Payload are real under the pre-registered gate but small (r~0.06–0.08)—useful as existence under control, not as a large effect to oversell. Tears of Steel null + residualization together say: film matter; Payload hits aren’t just visual leakage into the target.
Multi-item brain 30/50 network items DECODES on Payload vs 5/50 on ToS reframes the project from “one valence item” to “which continuous constructs the cortex carries under naturalistic viewing.”
Why no p-values Annotations autocorrelate for tens of seconds on short films; liberal second-level tests overstate precision. Film-block CIs (text) and shift-null gates (brain) are the honest inferential language of this design.

Recommended paper strategy

One integrated paper first (LLM multi-item validation + gated multi-subject brain decode). Trendy intersection for 2025–26; splitting weakens both halves (text alone = modest LM paper; brain alone = n=5×2).

Candidate titles (pick one spine)

  1. Primary: Dialogue-based language models are weak continuous emotion labels for naturalistic film: multi-item human agreement and gated multi-subject BOLD decoding in Emo-FilM
  2. Validating transformer valence labels against 50 continuous human ratings and affective-cortex decoding in open film fMRI
  3. Where automatic emotion labels fail—and where small affective BOLD signals survive—in continuous naturalistic annotation
  4. Effect-size reporting for continuous film emotion: transformers, multi-item GRID annotations, and controlled ROI decoding (methods-leaning)
  5. Not just valence: multi-item continuous annotation changes both NLP agreement and affective BOLD maps in Emo-FilM (multi-item lean)

Journal targets & estimated odds

“Odds” = rough probability of eventual acceptance after typical revision path, assuming clean write-up, honest limitations, and no new fatal bugs—not desk-reject-only odds. SSCI vs SCIE: many top neuro journals are SCIE; several dual-indexed. We flag indexing family.

Recommended first submission: Imaging Neuroscience or NeuroImage with the integrated title #1, full limitations box (n=5×2, small r, dialogue-only text, no p-values), multi-item + residualization as differentiators. Parallel “plan B” draft for BRM or IEEE TAC if neuro venues desk-reject on sample size.

What raises odds (do before submit)

  • One crystal-clear figure of merit narrative (text floor + brain gates).
  • Pre-registration language for FD subjects + ROI set + DECODES rule (even if post-hoc documented).
  • SI: full 50-item tables, per-film forests, all ROI multi-item hits (already in repo JSON).
  • Do not claim population generalizability from 2 films.
  • Dual-author contribution statement (Jessica / Albert roles).

What lowers odds (avoid)

  • Selling r≈0.07 as a large “emotion decoder.”
  • p-hacking continuous series.
  • Omitting Tears of Steel nulls.
  • Presenting as single-author when it is jointly Albert & Jessica’s project.
