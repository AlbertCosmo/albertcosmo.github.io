Primary dataset (not ours)

Emo-FilM (Morgenroth et al., 2025, Scientific Data): 14 short Creative Commons films, continuous multi-item emotion annotations, fMRI, respiration, heart rate, EDA.

  • Annotations: OpenNeuro ds004872 (CC0)
  • fMRI / physio: OpenNeuro ds004892 (CC0; full release ~131 GB)
  • Paper DOI: 10.1038/s41597-025-04803-5

What this project keeps in git

  • Processed text signals and 50-item human series (JSON under data/transcripts/; 14 films × 50 items).
  • 1 Hz visual features for Payload & TearsOfSteel; audio features for TearsOfSteel.
  • Result tables under results/ and results/publication_tables/; publication figures under figures/pub/.
  • Analysis code, download scripts, documentation (including this site’s source numbers).

What is local-only (not redistributed)

  • Preprocessed BOLD NIfTIs — obtain via OpenNeuro / scripts/download_openneuro_subset.sh (~9 GB subset for the 10 runs we use).
  • ROI decode caches (data/decode_cache*, *.npz voxel matrices) — gitignored.
  • Experiment film cuts (*_exp.mp4 / Movies_cut) — not on OpenNeuro; needed only for full multimodal on non-CC titles.

Film media

Tears of Steel is freely downloadable (Blender Open Movie, CC-BY). Script: scripts/download_films_cc.sh. Other titles in Emo-FilM are CC-licensed but experiment-specific cuts (credits removed, exact onsets) may differ from public masters — restore from the original media archive if you need bit-identical stimuli.
