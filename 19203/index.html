03

Every Meeting

The same two hours, in the same order, every week. You will always know what is coming, so you can spend your attention on the English rather than on the schedule.

Hour 1

Conversational Practice

  • Warm-up questions on this week’s theme.
  • Speaking in pairs and small groups, with partners changing week to week.
  • A short individual turn in front of the group.
  • Collect the words you needed but did not have.
Hour 2

E-book Practice

  • Work through this week’s unit in the e-book.
  • Listening and reading tasks done here in class, under time.
  • Check answers together, then find where in the text each answer was.
  • Carry the unit’s language back into next week’s first hour.

05

How to Label Your Work

Two formats, used every time. Work that arrives unlabelled or wrongly labelled is work I cannot give back to you, so this matters more than it looks.

Recording file names

ID1_NAME1_ID2_NAME2_ID3_NAME3

Every member of the group, in the same order every time: student ID, then name, separated by underscores. No spaces, no brackets, no week numbers.

For example  11234567_Chen_Yi-Hsuan_11234568_Lin_Wei_11234569_Wang_Mei-Ling

Presentation slides

Group NUMBER

Put your group number on the deck, and on each individual slide put the name and student ID of the member presenting that slide. I should be able to look at any one slide and know who is speaking to it.

For example  Group 3  ·  slide footer: Chen Yi-Hsuan, 11234567
