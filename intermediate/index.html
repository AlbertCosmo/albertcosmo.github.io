03

Objectives

This course builds vocabulary and everyday English usage through popular films and TV series. Over the semester you will:

  • Improve speaking and listening. Follow real dialogue at natural speed and respond to it.
  • Learn practical colloquial English. The expressions people actually use, not only the ones in textbooks.
  • Practise pronunciation and intonation. Including accents you will meet outside the classroom.
  • Understand American and British cultures. The context that makes the jokes, the silences, and the arguments make sense.
04

Every Meeting

The same three moves, in the same order, every week. You will always know what is coming, so you can spend your attention on the English rather than on the schedule.

Move 1

Listening Comprehension

  • Watch the week's clips from the film or series.
  • Follow the dialogue closely enough to identify theme, tone, and intent.
  • Collect the words and expressions you needed but did not have.
Move 2

Discussion

  • Speak about the week's question in pairs and then to the room.
  • Use the new vocabulary out loud while it is still fresh.
  • Take notes on what your classmates say, not only on what I say.
Move 3

Debate

  • Take a side on the week's motion, whether or not it is the side you hold.
  • Defend it with evidence from the clip.
  • This is the rehearsal for your midterm and final presentations.

Requirements and Grading

You are expected to take an active part in class: watching the clips, practising the conversations, and taking notes. Participation is not a bonus here, it is a fifth of the grade.

Breakdown 100% plus 5% bonus
20%

Recordings

In pairs, record yourselves speaking for about two minutes on the material covered in class. Five recordings in total, each worth 4%. Each one should show real engagement with the film or series, not a summary read aloud.

30%

Midterm Project

Present a proposal for your final project. State your topic, explain why it belongs in this course, and name the film or TV show you will analyse.

30%

Final Project Presentation

In groups, deliver a presentation in English that pulls your research or analysis together. Slides and other aids are welcome.

20%

Attendance and Discussion

Being in the room, and speaking while you are in it.

+5%

Final Reflective Essay (optional bonus)

Reflect on your semester: what you learned, what you enjoyed, and how the final project changed your understanding or your skills. Worth up to 5 bonus points on your final grade.

07

Class Rules and Policies

Attendance

Full attendance is strongly encouraged, in line with university policy. Unexcused absences are cumulative:

  • First unexcused absence: 10% deducted from your total score.
  • Second: a further 10% deducted.
  • Third: you fail the course.

To avoid these penalties, report the absence through the school system and email me at least 24 hours before the class, with valid documentation.

Plagiarism and generative AI

Plagiarism results in failure of this course.

AI-generated content may not be used in essays, writing assignments, or speeches for this course. Submitting AI-generated content counts as academic dishonesty: it means an automatic failure of that assignment, and repeated cases can mean failure of the course. Your work must begin as your own writing, not as an AI draft you then edit.

For the university guidelines on generative AI and AI-assisted technologies, see english.fju.edu.tw/faculty_area_detail.asp?OFT_No=34.
