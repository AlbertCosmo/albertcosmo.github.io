First, add this to your Home Screen

  1. In Safari, tap Share (the square with an arrow).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Roll Call from the new icon and enter your passcode there.

The Home Screen app keeps its own storage, separate from Safari. Rosters unlocked in a Safari tab won't carry over, and Safari may clear site data after 7 days without a visit.
